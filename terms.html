Plain English, because nobody reads the other kind. By using Judith you are agreeing to what follows.

The short version. Judith is free to use and early in its life. Your data belongs to you. Cook sensibly, don’t use it for anything harmful, and don’t rely on it as the only copy of something you would hate to lose.

What Judith is

A meal planning app for households: weekly plans, shopping lists, prep schedules, recipes, timers, and some features that use artificial intelligence.

Your account

You need to be 13 or older to make an account. Children under 13 can be added as profiles by an adult — a name with no login.

Keep your password to yourself. Whoever creates a household runs it, and decides who is invited and who is removed.

Your data is yours

Recipes, notes, photos and plans you put into Judith remain yours. We do not claim ownership and will not sell them, publish them, or use them to train anything.

We store them so your household can use them across devices, and that is all.

Cooking safely is up to you

Judith helps with timing and organisation. It does not know whether your chicken is cooked.

  • Recipes produced by the AI features are generated, not tested. Read them before you cook them.
  • Nutrition figures are rough estimates from a small built-in table. They are not medical or dietary advice.
  • Timers only sound while the app is open on screen.
  • Use a thermometer, follow proper food safety, and use your own judgement about allergies.

Preserving recipes

Recovering a dish from memory produces a best guess, and Judith tells you which parts it filled in. It is a starting point for you to correct, not a record of what your grandmother actually did.

What we ask of you

  • Do not use Judith to break the law or to harm anyone
  • Do not upload other people’s copyrighted recipe collections wholesale
  • Do not try to break into other households’ data
  • Do not abuse the AI features in a way that runs up costs for everyone

It is a beta

Judith is early software built by one person. It may have bugs, it may lose data, and features may change or disappear. It is provided as it is, with no guarantee that it will work or keep working.

Keep your own backups. Settings → Back up gives you a file with everything in it.

If money is ever involved

Judith is free today. If paid features arrive, everything free at the time you signed up stays free for you. Prices, refunds and cancellation will be set out clearly before anyone is asked to pay.

Ending it

You can stop using Judith whenever you like and take your data with you. We can close an account that is being used to harm others. If Judith shuts down, you will be given notice and time to export.
