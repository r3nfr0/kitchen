Privacy

Last updated 20 August 2026 · beta

Judith is a meal planning app for families. This page says what it stores, where it goes, and what it never does. It is written to be read, not skipped.

The short version. Your recipes, meal plans and shopping lists are yours. They are stored so your household can share them across your devices. They are never sold, never used for advertising, and never shown to anyone outside your household.

What Judith stores

When you make an account we keep your email address and a password you never share with us — sign-in is handled by Google Firebase, and the password itself is never visible to us.

Inside your household, Judith stores what you put there:

  • Recipes, ingredients, methods, ratings and notes
  • Your weekly meal plans and special meals
  • Grocery lists and which shop you buy things at
  • Prices you enter or scan from a receipt
  • How many adults and children are in the household, and the names of any profiles you create
  • A record of who added or changed what, so the family can see

What never leaves your device

  • Recipe photos. They stay on the phone or tablet that took them.
  • Receipt images. The text is read on your device. The picture is never uploaded.
  • Timers. Kept locally.

Usage information

Judith records which screens and buttons get used and for how long, so it can be improved around what people actually do. That record contains counts and timings only.

It never contains the contents of your recipes, your notes, your photos, your shopping list, or anyone’s name. Your rough region comes from your device’s time zone — not from GPS, and not from your address.

When you use the AI features

Ask Judith, Mix it up, recovering a remembered recipe, and the assistant all send your question to Google’s Gemini through a relay we run. What gets sent is your question and the context that makes the answer useful — the recipe you are looking at, what is in your pantry, how many you cook for.

Your email address is not sent. Your name is not sent. On the free tier, Google may use prompts to improve their products, as described in their own terms. If that matters to you, do not put anything private into those boxes.

Children

Judith does not create accounts for children. A child in your household is a profile — a name you type, with no email, no password and no login. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Who else is involved

  • Google Firebase — sign-in and storing your household’s data
  • Cloudflare — the relay that carries AI requests
  • Google Gemini — answering AI questions
  • OpenStreetMap — looking up shops near a town you type in
  • GitHub — serving the app itself

There are no advertising networks, tracking pixels or analytics companies involved.

Getting your data out, or deleting it

Settings → Back up gives you a file containing everything in your household. It is yours to keep.

To delete your account and its data, email the address below and it will be removed. If Judith is ever shut down, you will be given notice and time to export first.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, you will be told in the app rather than quietly.
